# Algebra — High school

Covers Algebra I and II. Visuals use the Cartesian plane wherever there is something to plot.

Rolls up to: string theory and computers (see [connections](../../connections.md)).

| # | Concept | Key ideas | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Variables, expressions & equations | A letter stands for an unknown or any number; an equation claims two sides are equal; doing the same thing to both sides keeps it true | — |
| 2 | The Cartesian plane & functions | Points are number pairs; a function gives one output per input; slope is the change in y per step in x and `b` is where the line starts | — |
| 3 | Systems & inequalities | A system's solution is where the graphs cross; an inequality is a whole region; multiplying by a negative flips the sign | — |
| 4 | Polynomials & quadratics | Factoring turns a polynomial into a rectangle's sides; roots are where the parabola crosses the x-axis; the quadratic formula comes from completing the square | — |
| 5 | Exponentials & logarithms | Exponential growth multiplies by the same amount each step and overtakes any linear growth; a logarithm undoes an exponential | — |
| 6 | Function families & transformations | Every graph belongs to a family; changing numbers in the equation shifts, stretches, or flips it in predictable ways | — |
