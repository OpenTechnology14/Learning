# Algebra — High school

Covers Algebra I and II. Every concept is drawn on the Cartesian plane.

Rolls up to: string theory and computers (see [connections](../../connections.md)).

| # | Concept | Whiteboard | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Variables, expressions & equations | Balance scale solving `3x + 2 = 11`, same step on both pans; beside it, `y = 3x + 2` meeting `y = 11` at `x = 3` on a grid; note: "an expression is a value, an equation is a claim" | — |
| 2 | The Cartesian plane & functions | Descartes' grid with a point `(x, y)`; note: "every equation is a picture"; then `y = mx + b` with the rise/run staircase and `b` circled | — |
| 3 | Systems & inequalities | Two lines crossing with the solution point circled; a shaded half-plane with a dashed vs. solid boundary | — |
| 4 | Polynomials & quadratics | Algebra tiles forming a rectangle (factoring), then a parabola with roots, vertex, and the quadratic formula | — |
| 5 | Exponentials & logarithms | +2 each step vs. ×2 each step columns and curves; log drawn as the mirror of the exponential across `y = x` | — |
| 6 | Function families & transformations | Parent graphs (linear, quadratic, radical, rational) with shifted/stretched copies; note: "`f(x − h) + k` moves right h, up k" | — |
