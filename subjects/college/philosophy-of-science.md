# Philosophy of Science — College

| # | Concept | Key ideas | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Demarcation & the scientific method | What separates science from non-science; science makes predictions that could turn out wrong and tests them | — |
| 2 | Induction & confirmation | Past observations can't prove the future (Hume); a theory can be refuted but never fully proven (Popper); evidence raises or lowers confidence (Bayes) | — |
| 3 | Theory change | Science runs inside a framework until anomalies pile up and force a new one (Kuhn); theories have a protected core (Lakatos) | — |
| 4 | Evidence & objectivity | A failed test doesn't say which assumption broke; what we observe depends on what we already believe; values affect how much evidence is enough | — |
| 5 | Realism vs. anti-realism | Whether science describes what's really there or only gives useful predictions; the arguments on each side | — |
| 6 | Explanation, causation & laws | Explaining is more than predicting; correlation isn't causation; laws differ from accidental regularities | — |
| 7 | Reductionism & emergence | Whether each science reduces to the one below it, or new behavior emerges at higher levels | — |
