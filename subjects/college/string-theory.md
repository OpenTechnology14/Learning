# String Theory — College capstone

Where the math track ends on the physics side: relativity's geometry, quantum
mechanics, and Noether's symmetries pushed into one theory. Still unconfirmed
by experiment; students should know why. See
[connections](../../connections.md).

| # | Concept | Key ideas | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why the two pillars clash | General relativity and quantum mechanics each work alone but give nonsense when combined at the smallest scales | — |
| 2 | Particles as vibrating strings | Different particles are different vibration patterns of one kind of string; one pattern behaves like the graviton | — |
| 3 | Extra dimensions | Extra dimensions can be curled up too small to see; the theory needs 10 dimensions to be consistent | — |
| 4 | Supersymmetry & superstrings | Supersymmetry pairs every force particle with a matter particle; it made string theory mathematically consistent | — |
| 5 | Branes, dualities & M-theory | The five versions of string theory turn out to be views of one larger theory, M-theory | — |
| 6 | Holography | The information inside a region can be fully described on its boundary, as with a black hole's surface | — |
| 7 | Testability & criticism | No experiment has confirmed it yet; why testing is so hard; why some call it not yet science | — |
