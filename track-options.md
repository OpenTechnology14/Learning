## Physics

1. ★ **Unification** – Newton puts heaven and earth under one law of gravity →
   Maxwell unifies electricity, magnetism, and light (1865) → Einstein unifies
   space and time (1905) and gravity with geometry (1915) → electroweak
   unification and the Standard Model (1960s–70s).
   *Rolls up to:* String Theory. *Connects to:* math (calculus, geometry).
2. **Energy & the arrow of time** – Carnot's steam-engine limit (1824) →
   Clausius names entropy (1865) → Boltzmann counts atoms to explain it →
   Planck's quanta (1900) → black holes have entropy (Bekenstein & Hawking,
   1970s).
   *Rolls up to:* String Theory (holography) or Computers & AI (information).
   *Connects to:* engineering, chemistry.
3. **Light & matter** – Newton's optics → Young's double slit shows light is a
   wave (1801) → Einstein's photon (1905) → quantum mechanics (Bohr,
   Heisenberg, Schrödinger, 1913–26) → lasers and the transistor (1947) →
   quantum computers.
   *Rolls up to:* Computers & AI. *Connects to:* chemistry (spectra),
   engineering (electronics).

## Chemistry

1. ★ **From elements to life** – Lavoisier's conservation of mass (1789) →
   Dalton's atoms (1808) → Wöhler makes an organic compound from inorganic
   ones (1828) → Mendeleev's periodic table (1869) → Lewis's shared-electron
   bond (1916) → biochemistry and DNA.
   *Rolls up to:* modern health (drugs, biochemistry). *Connects to:* biology.
2. **Quantum chemistry** – periodic table → electrons in orbitals → Pauling's
   *Nature of the Chemical Bond* (1939) → spectroscopy → computer-simulated
   molecules → AI-designed molecules and materials.
   *Rolls up to:* Computers & AI. *Connects to:* physics.
3. **Materials & energy** – fire and combustion → Volta's battery (1800) →
   Haber–Bosch fertilizer feeds billions (1909–13) → plastics and polymers →
   lithium-ion batteries (Nobel 2019) → carbon and climate.
   *Rolls up to:* engineering and climate. *Connects to:* Earth & Space.

## Engineering

1. **Power** – Watt's steam engine (1769) → Faraday's generator principle
   (1831) → the electric grid (1880s) → engines and flight → renewables and
   the modern grid.
   *Rolls up to:* modern energy and climate. *Connects to:* physics
   (thermodynamics), chemistry (batteries).
2. **Structures** – the Roman arch → iron and steel → the Brooklyn Bridge
   (1883) → skyscrapers → learning from failure (Tacoma Narrows, 1940) →
   computer simulation of every part before it's built.
   *Rolls up to:* Computers & AI (simulation). *Connects to:* classical
   mechanics.
3. ★ **Control & information** – Watt's governor, the first feedback machine
   (1788) → Maxwell's math of governors (1868) → telegraph and telephone →
   transistor (1947) → integrated circuit (1958–59) → robots and self-driving
   systems.
   *Rolls up to:* Computers & AI. *Connects to:* math, physics.

## Philosophy

Biology already rolls up into philosophy; these are where philosophy goes next.

1. ★ **Logic → computers** – Aristotle's syllogisms → Boole's algebra of logic
   (1854) → Frege's formal logic (1879) → Russell → Gödel (1931) → Turing
   (1936) → AI.
   *Rolls up to:* Computers & AI. *Connects to:* math.
2. **Mind** – Descartes splits mind and body (the same Descartes as the
   Cartesian plane) → Locke and Hume: knowledge from experience → Kant →
   behaviorism → cognitive science → the Turing test (1950) → can AI be
   conscious?
   *Rolls up to:* Computers & AI. *Connects to:* biology (brain).
3. **Knowledge & science** – Socrates and Plato → Bacon's scientific method
   (1620) → Hume's problem of induction → Kant → Popper and Kuhn → ethics of
   modern science (bioethics, AI ethics).
   *Rolls up to:* Philosophy of Science and ethics. *Connects to:* String
   Theory ("is it science yet?"), modern health.

## Earth & Space Science

1. ★ **Cosmic origins** – Copernicus moves the Sun to the center (1543) →
   Kepler and Galileo → Newton's gravity → Hubble's expanding universe (1929)
   → Big Bang afterglow found (1965) → dark matter and dark energy.
   *Rolls up to:* String Theory (the early universe). *Connects to:* physics.
2. **Deep time** – Hutton's ancient Earth (1788) → Lyell's geology, which
   Darwin carried on the Beagle → radiometric dating → plate tectonics
   (Wegener 1912, accepted 1960s).
   *Rolls up to:* biology (evolution needs deep time). *Connects to:*
   chemistry (dating).
3. **Climate** – Fourier's heat-trapping atmosphere (1824) → Tyndall measures
   CO₂ absorbing heat (1859) → Arrhenius predicts warming (1896) → Keeling's
   CO₂ curve (1958) → computer climate models.
   *Rolls up to:* modern health and energy. *Connects to:* chemistry,
   Computers & AI.
